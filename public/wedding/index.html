Q&A

What time should I arrive?

Cocktail hour starts at 6:00 PM — come then, grab a drink and some hors d’oeuvres, and settle in. The reception and dinner begin at 7:00 PM.

What’s for dinner and drinks?

A buffet dinner, a champagne toast, wedding cake and sweets, and a top-shelf open bar through the evening — all hosted by us. Just bring your appetite and your dancing shoes.

Can I bring a plus-one or the kids?

Your formal invitation will name everyone included in your party. If you’re unsure, reach out to Arsen or Aidai and we’ll sort it out.

How do I RSVP?

Please fill out our RSVP form by Monday, December 7, 2026. If anything changes after that, just reach out to Arsen or Aidai directly.

Is there a registry?

Your presence really is the present. Registry details will be added here once they’re set up.
